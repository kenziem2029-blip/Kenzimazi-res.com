Cement cakes

They're really soft :)

Ingredients

-400 grams of cement

-7 cups of water

-9 sticks of butter

-2 grams of sugar

-50 pounds of food coloring

-330 grams of flour

-20 grams of clay

-5 large eggs

Steps!!!

1) Put the flour and cement in a big mixing bowl and stir together

2) Add the sticks of butter and punch the goop until it is fully mixed

3) Add half the water and hula hoop for an hour

4) When you come back add the rest of the water and the clay

5) Put in the microwave for 80 minutes

6) Mix the sugar, food coloring, and eggs

7) Take the cake out of the microwave and spread the sugar mixture on top

8) Write a cute note and give to one of your favorite people to eat

9) When they ask for you to pay their medical bills run away
